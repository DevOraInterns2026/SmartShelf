# SmartShelf

> Smart Inventory Management System for Shops

SmartShelf is a smart inventory management system designed to help shops manage their products, monitor stock levels, and organize their inventory efficiently.

Developed as part of the **DevOra Internship Program**.

---

## Overview

SmartShelf provides a structured workflow for managing shop inventory:

```text
Product Management
      ↓
Inventory Tracking
      ↓
Stock Monitoring
      ↓
Low-Stock Detection
      ↓
Smart Alerts
      ↓
Inventory Management
      ↓
Reports & Insights
```

---

## Features

* 📦 Product management
* 📊 Inventory tracking
* ⚠️ Low-stock monitoring
* 🔔 Stock alerts
* 🔎 Product search and filtering
* 📈 Inventory reports
* 🤖 Smart inventory analysis
* 🖥️ User-friendly interface

---

## Project Goal

The main goal of **SmartShelf** is to make inventory management easier, faster, and more organized for shop owners.

The system helps reduce manual inventory work and provides better visibility into product stock levels.
